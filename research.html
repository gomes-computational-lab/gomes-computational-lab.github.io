Gomes Computational Lab

Research & Students

The Gomes Computational Lab develops artificial intelligence, machine learning, and high-performance computing methods for healthcare and biomedical research. Our work spans medical imaging, cancer genomics, clinical decision support, and AI-enabled healthcare systems, with additional interdisciplinary collaborations in scientific and geospatial computing.
