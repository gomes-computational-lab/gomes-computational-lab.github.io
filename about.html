Professional Profile

I am an Associate Professor and Chair of Computer Science at the University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire. My work focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and high-performance computing, with applications in healthcare, scientific computing, and precision agriculture.

I lead NSF-supported undergraduate research, collaborate with Mayo Clinic’s AI & Informatics team, and help build AI curricula, institutional strategy, and research cyberinfrastructure.
